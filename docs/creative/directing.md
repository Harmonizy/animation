# Directing, lighting, color and sound

**Status: guidance only (Planned).** No lighting or audio features exist. Sources:
[references](../REFERENCES.md).

## Directing questions

For each shot:

1. What must the audience understand or feel here?
2. Where should they look first?
3. What is the simplest image that achieves that?
4. How does it connect to the shots before and after?

For each sequence, decide who it belongs to emotionally, where the turn happens, and where the pacing
should breathe or tighten.

## Lighting

- **Key, fill, rim:** main light, shadow softening, and edge separation from the background.
- **High-key:** bright with low contrast. It is often open or light in mood, but not always.
- **Low-key:** strong shadows, often used for tension or intimacy.
- **Motivated lighting:** appears to come from a believable source in the scene.

## Color and mood

- **Color script:** a planned sequence of color across the whole film, tracking emotional shifts.
- **Contrast:** place the strongest value contrast where attention should go.
- **Atmosphere:** haze and fog reduce contrast with distance and help separate depth.

## Sound

| Layer      | Role                                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Ambience   | Establishes place, and can carry mood             |
| Foley      | Physical presence of bodies and objects           |
| Dialogue   | Information and character                         |
| Music      | Emotional direction, often strongest when sparing |
| Sound cues | Time-specific events that need precise sync       |

Silence is also a choice. Audio sync is planned around the same timeline clock as visuals (Phase 5).
