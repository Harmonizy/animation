## Not finished, and why

1. **Narration.** This environment cannot reach a Thai text-to-speech service without a key, and a
   narration track is never faked. `npm run narration:episode` makes it once `GOOGLE_TTS_API_KEY` is
   set; then rerun `audio:episode` and `render:episode`. The request code was only tested with
   `--dry-run`, not against the live API.
2. **Watching and listening at speed.** Frames, levels and timings were checked, but nobody has yet
   watched the film in motion with sound. Do this before publishing.
3. **Licensing of the result.** Music and effects are generated by code in this repository, the
   typeface is Noto Sans Thai under the SIL Open Font License, and no external images are used. A voice
   made with Google Cloud is subject to Google's terms for the generated audio.
